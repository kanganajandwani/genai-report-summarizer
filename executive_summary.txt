EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Infosys ESG Report 2024–25

The Infosys ESG Report 2024–25 presents the company’s progress and priorities across three main areas: Environment, Social and Governance. The report shows that Infosys is focusing on sustainability not only through environmental targets, but also through employee development, digital inclusion, responsible technology, data privacy and ethical governance.

Environment

A major achievement highlighted in the report is that Infosys remained carbon neutral for the sixth consecutive year across its global operations. The company is also working towards becoming climate positive by 2030. Its environmental strategy focuses on reducing energy consumption, increasing renewable energy use, improving water management and reducing waste.

For India operations, 77.7% of electricity came from renewable sources. Infosys also reported 29.7 million square feet of the highest-level green certified space. Water management is another important area, with 40 lakes across campuses providing a combined rainwater storage capacity of around 430 million liters. The company reported 100% recycling of wastewater and 98% of waste being diverted from landfills.

Infosys has also continued carbon-offset projects that benefit more than 2,72,000 rural families. These projects are aimed at improving access to cleaner energy while also supporting communities. The company’s environmental direction therefore combines operational improvements with projects intended to create wider environmental and social benefits.

Social Impact and People

The report places significant emphasis on people, digital skills and inclusion. Infosys reached 13.3 million people with digital skills, including employees, clients’ workforce, students, teachers and communities. Its Tech for Good initiatives have impacted more than 125 million lives through areas such as education, healthcare and e-governance.

Employee diversity is another important part of the report. Women represented 39% of the workforce. Infosys was also recognized as a Global Top Employer in 22 countries for its human-resource practices and processes. During the year, more than 15,200 fresh graduates were hired globally.

The company also reported 1,30,000+ volunteering hours through InfyCares, with more than 34,000 employees participating across 21 locations. These figures show that the social part of the ESG strategy extends beyond employees to communities and wider society.

Governance and Responsible Technology

Governance is increasingly connected with technology-related responsibilities. The report highlights data privacy, cybersecurity, information management and responsible use of artificial intelligence as important areas. Infosys received ISO 42001:2023 certification for AI management systems, showing its focus on establishing structured practices for managing AI responsibly.

Another significant milestone was Infosys becoming the first India-headquartered company to receive approval for Binding Corporate Rules from EU data protection authorities. This reflects the company’s focus on data privacy and compliance across geographies.

Infosys was also recognized among the World’s Most Ethical Companies by Ethisphere for the fifth consecutive year. This supports the company’s broader emphasis on ethical business practices and governance.

Strategic Direction

Overall, the report shows that Infosys is moving from maintaining ESG performance towards setting more ambitious long-term goals. The company’s next major environmental goal is to become climate positive by 2030 while continuing its carbon-neutral journey. At the same time, it aims to expand digital skilling, strengthen workplace inclusion, support communities and maintain high standards in data privacy, information security and AI governance.

From a business perspective, the report suggests that ESG is being treated as part of long-term value creation rather than as a separate activity. Environmental efficiency can reduce resource use, digital-skilling programs can build opportunities for people, and stronger governance can support trust in an increasingly AI-driven business environment.

Conclusion

The Infosys ESG Report 2024–25 shows progress across environmental, social and governance priorities. Key achievements include six consecutive years of carbon neutrality, 77.7% renewable electricity in India operations, 13.3 million people reached through digital skilling, 39% women in the workforce, and continued recognition for ethical and responsible business practices. The main direction going forward is to build on these achievements while working towards climate positivity, wider digital inclusion and stronger responsible-technology practices.

Source: Infosys ESG Report 2024–25.
