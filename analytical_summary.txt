ANALYTICAL SUMMARY
Infosys ESG Report 2024–25

1. ENVIRONMENT

• Carbon neutrality:
  - Infosys remained carbon neutral for the sixth consecutive year.
  - Long-term target: become climate positive by 2030.

• Renewable energy:
  - 77.7% of electricity used by India operations came from renewable sources.

• Green buildings:
  - 29.7 million sq. ft. of Infosys space was covered by the highest-level green certification.

• Water management:
  - 40 lakes were developed across Infosys campuses.
  - Combined rainwater storage capacity: approximately 430 million litres.
  - 100% of wastewater was recycled.

• Waste management:
  - 98% of waste was diverted from landfills.

• Community-linked environmental projects:
  - Carbon-offset projects benefited more than 2,72,000 rural families.
  - These initiatives combine environmental benefits with community support.

2. SOCIAL AND PEOPLE

• Digital skills:
  - 13.3 million people were reached through digital-skilling initiatives.
  - The programs covered employees, students, teachers, clients' workforce and communities.

• Tech for Good:
  - Infosys reported an impact on more than 125 million lives through initiatives in areas such as education, healthcare and e-governance.

• Gender diversity:
  - Women represented 39% of the workforce.

• Hiring:
  - More than 15,200 fresh graduates were hired globally during the year.

• Employee recognition:
  - Infosys was recognized as a Global Top Employer in 22 countries.

• Volunteering:
  - More than 1,30,000 volunteering hours were recorded through InfyCares.
  - More than 34,000 employees participated across 21 locations.

3. GOVERNANCE AND RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

• AI governance:
  - Infosys received ISO 42001:2023 certification for AI management systems.
  - This supports a structured approach to responsible AI management.

• Data privacy:
  - Infosys became the first India-headquartered company to receive approval for Binding Corporate Rules from EU data protection authorities.

• Ethics:
  - Infosys was recognized among the World's Most Ethical Companies by Ethisphere for the fifth consecutive year.

• Key governance focus areas:
  - Data privacy
  - Cybersecurity
  - Information management
  - Responsible artificial intelligence
  - Ethical business practices

4. KEY TRENDS AND BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS

• Infosys is moving beyond short-term ESG activities towards longer-term sustainability goals, particularly the target of becoming climate positive by 2030.

• The high share of renewable electricity and continued investment in water and waste management indicate a focus on reducing the environmental impact of operations.

• Digital-skilling initiatives have a large social reach and support the company's wider technology and inclusion goals.

• The 39% representation of women shows progress in workforce diversity, while continued hiring of fresh graduates supports talent development.

• AI management certification, data privacy controls and ethical-business recognition indicate that governance is becoming increasingly important as technology use expands.

• The combination of environmental, social and governance initiatives shows that ESG is being connected with long-term business sustainability rather than being treated as a separate activity.
